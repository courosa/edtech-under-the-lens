18–20 minutes per activity · On your own or in a group of 2–4

18–20 min · Solo or group of 2–4
How it works

    Recommended: working & sharing
    • Allow 6–8 minutes to prepare, 45–60 seconds to present, and 4–5 minutes to discuss.
    • Short answers are welcome. Use the prompts that help your thinking; you can skip and return.
    • In a group, invite everyone’s ideas and have one person type.
    • Share your screen, read aloud, or contribute text. Choose what works for you.
    Make it your own
    • Edit the brief to fit your context. Keep the scenario fictional.
    • Add humour, fictional names, or a dramatic voice. Cameras and posting in chat are optional.
    • Start with your own ideas, write together, and build on what others share. No AI tools are needed.
    Facilitation notes
    • For variety, assign different briefs; random draws may repeat across groups.
    • Each group uses one browser. Drafts are not shared or synced.
    • After sharing, open the discussion guide. Anyone can lead the conversation.
    • This tool arranges your answers; it does not generate or assess them with AI.
